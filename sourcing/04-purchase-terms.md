# Standard Purchase Terms — Goyal Lights
Attach this to every purchase order you send to an overseas supplier.
Send it as a PDF annexure titled *"Annexure A — Standard Terms of Purchase"* and ask the
supplier to sign and stamp the last page. A signed annexure is what makes a claim
enforceable later; an email chain usually is not.

## 1. Prices

1.1 All prices are **FOB [named port], in USD**, unless the purchase order states CIF Nhava Sheva or CIF ICD Tughlakabad.
1.2 Quoted prices are **firm for 90 days** from the date of the supplier's quotation.
1.3 Any price increase requires **30 days' written notice** and does not apply to orders already confirmed.
1.4 The price includes export packing, marking, and all documentation listed in clause 6.
1.5 Bank charges: remitting bank charges are for the Buyer; receiving and intermediary bank charges are for the Supplier.

## 2. Minimum order quantity

2.1 MOQ is agreed **per model**, and stated in the supplier's price list.
2.2 MOQ for a colour temperature, body colour or voltage variant of a model is agreed separately and must be stated. A supplier who quotes "MOQ 1,000" and then requires 1,000 *per CCT* has quoted 3,000.
2.3 The Buyer may combine several models to reach a container or LCL consolidation; the Supplier will not refuse a mixed load that meets the per-model MOQ.
2.4 Trial orders below MOQ may be accepted at an agreed premium, stated in writing before order confirmation.

## 3. Payment

3.1 **First order:** 30% by T/T on order confirmation; 70% by T/T against a scanned copy of the Bill of Lading and a passed pre-shipment inspection.
3.2 **From the third order:** 20% advance / 80% against B/L, or an irrevocable L/C at sight, at the Buyer's option.
3.3 The Buyer does **not** pay 100% in advance, and does not pay before goods exist.
3.4 Payment is made **only** to a bank account held in the Supplier's exact registered company name, as shown on the business licence and the proforma invoice. Payment to a personal account, or to an account in a different name or a third country, will not be made.
3.5 **Any change to bank details mid-order must be confirmed by video call before payment.** An emailed change of bank details will be treated as fraud until verified.
3.6 All payments are in USD by T/T unless otherwise agreed in writing.

## 4. Product compliance — India

4.1 The Supplier warrants that every model supplied that falls under India's Compulsory Registration Scheme — LED bulbs (IS 16102), LED luminaires (IS 10322:2026 series), LED lighting chains, rope lights and decorative strings (IS 10322 Part 5 Sec 7) — **holds a valid BIS CRS R-number in the Supplier's factory name for that exact model**, and will provide the certificate before shipment.
4.2 Lamps must carry the correct **BEE star label** data, and the Supplier will provide tested lumen-per-watt figures.
4.3 Retail packaging must show country of origin and leave a clear panel for the Indian importer label (importer name and address, MRP, month and year of import, net quantity, customer care details) as required under the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules.
4.4 Products must be rated **220–240V, 50Hz**, with Indian-standard bases (B22 and E27 as specified).
4.5 **If goods are detained, rejected, confiscated or destroyed by Indian customs or BIS because of a compliance failure on the Supplier's side, the Supplier bears the full cost** — goods value, freight, demurrage, detention and destruction charges — and refunds all payments made.

## 5. Quality and inspection

5.1 Inspection standard: **AQL 2.5 for major defects, AQL 4.0 for minor defects** (ISO 2859-1 / MIL-STD-105E, normal level II).
5.2 The Buyer or the Buyer's appointed third-party inspector (SGS, TÜV, QIMA, Bureau Veritas or the Buyer's agent) may inspect at the Supplier's factory before shipment, at the Buyer's cost. The Supplier will give access and provide the goods packed and ready 3 working days before the shipment date.
5.3 **Balance payment is released only after a passed inspection.** A failed inspection is re-worked and re-inspected at the Supplier's cost.
5.4 The shipped goods must match the approved sample. Any change of component — driver, LED chip, housing material, wire gauge — requires the Buyer's written approval and a fresh approved sample.
5.5 **Warranty:** minimum 12 months from date of arrival for lamps and luminaires, 24 months where the Supplier's catalogue states it.
5.6 **Defect allowance:** dead-on-arrival rate up to 0.5% is absorbed by the Buyer. Above 0.5%, and for all failures within the warranty period, the Supplier issues a credit note against the next order or ships free replacements with the next consignment, at the Buyer's option. Photographic and video evidence will be provided by the Buyer within 30 days of arrival.
5.7 Claims are settled by credit note or free replacement, not by return shipment, unless the Supplier pays the return freight.

## 6. Packing, marking and documents

6.1 Export-standard 5-ply master cartons, sealed and strapped, suitable for 30+ days of sea transit and manual handling at an Indian ICD.
6.2 Each master carton marked with: model number, quantity, gross and net weight, carton dimensions, CBM, country of origin, and the Buyer's shipping mark.
6.3 Fragile items — glass shades, chandelier arms, filament bulbs — individually foam or moulded-pulp protected. Breakage above 1% on such items is to the Supplier's account.
6.4 **Documents to be couriered and emailed within 3 working days of shipment:** commercial invoice, packing list (with carton-wise breakdown), Bill of Lading, certificate of origin, BIS and BEE certificate copies, test reports, and any fumigation certificate required for wooden packing (ISPM-15).
6.5 Document errors cause customs delay and demurrage in India. **Demurrage and detention caused by incorrect or late documents is charged to the Supplier.**

## 7. Delivery

7.1 Lead time is stated on the order confirmation and runs from the date the advance payment is received.
7.2 The Supplier notifies the Buyer in writing at least **7 days before** the goods are ready, so the Buyer's forwarder can book space.
7.3 **Late delivery:** more than 15 days late without written agreement — the Buyer may claim 0.5% of the order value per week of delay, capped at 5%. More than 45 days late — the Buyer may cancel and recover the advance in full.
7.4 Risk and title pass at the ship's rail at the named port of loading (FOB, Incoterms 2020), or as per the Incoterm stated on the order.
7.5 The Buyer nominates the freight forwarder unless the order is placed on CIF terms.
7.6 Partial shipments and transhipment are permitted only with the Buyer's written consent.

## 8. Confidentiality and channel

8.1 Designs, drawings, specifications and private-label artwork supplied by the Buyer remain the Buyer's property and may not be shown, sold or supplied to any other buyer.
8.2 Private-label goods carrying the Goyal Lights brand may not be sold by the Supplier to any other party in any market.

## 9. General

9.1 The order confirmation, the price list, and this annexure together form the contract. Any variation must be in writing.
9.2 Force majeure: neither party is liable for delay caused by events genuinely outside its control, but the affected party must notify the other within 7 days and both parties will agree a revised schedule in writing.
9.3 Disputes to be settled first by negotiation. Failing that, the parties may refer the matter to arbitration; the seat and language are to be agreed in the order confirmation. Indian law applies to the Buyer's obligations in India.
9.4 The Supplier confirms it is the manufacturer / an authorised export agent of the manufacturer, and that the goods do not infringe any third-party design or trademark.
