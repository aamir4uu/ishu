## The five things that matter most

1. **Get GSTIN and IEC now.** Without GSTIN the 18% IGST at customs is a real cost, not a credit. Everything else is secondary to this.
2. **You cannot import Diwali 2026 stock from China — it is too late.** Buy it in India this season, record what sells, and use that record to buy from China in April–June 2027.
3. **BIS registration sits with the Chinese factory, not with you.** No R-number for that exact model, no clearance. Ask this in the first message, every time.
4. **Multiply the FOB rupee price by roughly 1.4** to know what a piece really costs on your shelf. Import only where the Delhi market price is at least 1.6× that.
5. **30% advance, 70% against the Bill of Lading.** Never 100% in advance, never into a personal account, never to changed bank details without a video call.
