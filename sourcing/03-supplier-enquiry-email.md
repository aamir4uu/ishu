**2. Information we need in your reply**

Please send your quotation as an Excel file with one row per model, containing:

1. **FOB price** in USD, with the port named (FOB Shenzhen / Ningbo / Guangzhou), and the price validity period
2. **MOQ per model**, and separately the MOQ per colour temperature and per body colour
3. **Price breaks** — your price at MOQ, at 3× MOQ and at full 20ft container quantity
4. **Packing data:** pcs per inner box, pcs per master carton, carton dimensions, CBM per carton, gross and net weight
5. **HS code** you declare on export
6. **Lead time** for the first order and for repeat orders
7. **Certifications held:** CE, RoHS, and specifically — **do you hold a valid BIS CRS R-number for the Indian market (IS 16102 for LED bulbs, IS 10322:2026 series for luminaires and lighting chains)? If yes, please send the certificate copy and the R-number.** This is mandatory for customs clearance in India and we cannot import a model without it.
8. **BEE star rating** data (lumens per watt, tested) for lamps
9. **Warranty period** offered and your procedure for defective goods
10. **Payment terms** you accept, and your bank details in the company's registered name
11. Whether you offer **OEM/private-label** printing on the product and the carton under our brand, and the MOQ for that
12. Your **catalogue** with photographs and technical data sheets

**3. Our terms — how we would like to work**

- **Trial order first.** One LCL shipment, 2–5 CBM, mixed models, so both sides can test the process. Repeat orders would grow to a 20ft container per quarter.

- **Inspection:** we reserve the right to appoint a third-party inspector (SGS / TÜV / QIMA / our own agent) for a pre-shipment inspection at your factory, at our cost. Balance payment follows a passed inspection. Please confirm you accept this.
- **Quality standard:** AQL 2.5 for major defects. Acceptable dead-on-arrival rate 0.5%. Anything above that to be credited against our next order or replaced free with the next shipment.
- **Packing and marking:** export-standard 5-ply master cartons, each carton marked with model, quantity, gross weight, CBM, country of origin and our shipping mark. Retail cartons must carry the country of origin and leave space for the Indian importer label (importer name and address, MRP, month and year of import) as required by Indian Legal Metrology rules.
- **Documents required with every shipment:** commercial invoice, packing list, Bill of Lading, certificate of origin, and the BIS/BEE certificate copies. Delay or error in documents causes demurrage in India, so document accuracy is part of the deal.

- **Prices:** we ask for a price hold of 90 days from confirmation, and 30 days' written notice of any increase.
- **Samples:** we will pay for samples and for courier. Please advise the sample cost and your DHL/FedEx account arrangement. We would like samples of items 1, 2 and 3 above to begin with.
- **Exclusivity:** if the first year goes well, we would like to discuss distribution rights for the Delhi NCR region.

## Sending notes

- **I could not send these emails for you** — this session has no email account connected. Copy each one into your own mail, or tell me which email service you use and I can look at what it would take to connect it.
- Use a business email address, not a personal Gmail with numbers in it. `sales@goyallights.com` or `goyallights@gmail.com` reads as a real buyer; `gaurav9899xyz@gmail.com` does not. A domain plus mailbox costs about ₹1,000/year and pays for itself the first time a factory takes you seriously.
- Attach nothing on the first email. Attachments from unknown Indian addresses go to spam in China.
- Put your **WhatsApp number in every message** — most Chinese lighting sales staff will move the conversation there immediately, and that is where the real negotiation happens.
