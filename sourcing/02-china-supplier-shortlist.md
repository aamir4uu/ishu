# China Supplier & Exporter Shortlist — Lighting
For Goyal Lights, Bhagirath Palace, Delhi · compiled 23 August 2026

> **Read this first.** Every address, phone and email below was taken from public
> company pages, trade-platform listings and business directories in August 2026.
> Chinese sales contacts and export desks change often. **Verify each one on the
> company's own website before you send money or samples**, and treat the general
> `info@` / `service@` addresses as a front door — the real relationship starts when
> a named salesperson replies and gives you their WeChat or WhatsApp.
>
> Do not pay any supplier into a personal account, into an account whose name does
> not exactly match the company name on the proforma invoice, or into a Hong Kong
> account for a mainland factory without asking why. Payment-detail changes sent by
> email mid-order are the single most common fraud in this trade — always confirm
> by phone or video call.

## B. Guzhen, Zhongshan — the decorative and fancy lighting capital
Guzhen town in Zhongshan is where the large majority of the world's decorative lighting is made: chandeliers, jhoomar, pendants, wall brackets, ceiling lights, magnetic track, profile. Thousands of small and medium factories in a few square kilometres. This is the right place for the "Fancy & Interior" half of your shop.

**How to work Guzhen:** these factories rarely have polished English websites. You find them on Made-in-China.com, Global Sources and Alibaba, or you walk the town during the fair. Open the platform storefront, read the company profile for years in business and export markets, then message the sales contact.

- `made-in-china.com/manufacturers/guzhen-lighting.html`
- Guzhen fair exhibitor list: `en.jiagle.com/lighting-fair/exhibitor-list/`

**Trading company vs factory:** for a first mixed order — 6 chandelier models, 4 pendant models, some track and some panels — a Guzhen *trading/export company* is often better than a factory. They consolidate from 20 factories into one container, handle the export paperwork, and accept small per-model quantities. You pay 5–10% more per piece and save far more than that on freight and on the headache of ten separate payments.

## D. Where to search, rather than who to email
For a shop your size, the platform search is more valuable than any fixed list — you want the factory that makes *your* fast-moving SKU, not the biggest name.

| Platform | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| **Alibaba.com** | Widest choice; use Trade Assurance so payment is escrowed. Filter: Verified Supplier, 5+ yrs, ships to India |
| **Made-in-China.com** | Stronger on real factories vs traders; Audited Supplier reports are detailed |
| **Global Sources** | Best for festive/seasonal ranges and OEM/ODM |
| **1688.com** | Domestic Chinese prices — 20–40% below export platforms — but Chinese only, no export service. Use with an agent |
| **Yiwugo / Yiwu market agents** | Small MOQ mixed festive and party lighting |

**Sourcing agents / consolidators** (3–5% commission, worth it for year one): search for agents based in Guzhen, Foshan, Zhongshan or Yiwu with lighting specialisation — e.g. HSY Sourcing, Foshan Sourcing, Minden Sourcing and the Yiwu agent market all publish lighting-specific pages. Interview three, ask for two Indian client references, and check they will do factory audits and loading supervision, not just quotes.

## E. Screening checklist — run this on every supplier before ordering

- [ ] Business licence copy received; company name matches the bank account name exactly
- [ ] Years in business ≥ 5, and export experience to India specifically
- [ ] **BIS CRS R-number held for the exact model** (mandatory for LED bulbs, luminaires, lighting chains) — certificate copy received and verified on the BIS site
- [ ] Factory address is a real factory (ask for a live video walk-through — genuine factories agree readily)
- [ ] Sample tested for 4+ hours: driver heat, flicker, actual lumens, solder quality, wire gauge, IP claim
- [ ] Quote is FOB with named port, and includes carton pcs, carton size, CBM, gross weight, HS code
- [ ] Written warranty terms and a defect/claim procedure
- [ ] Payment: 30% T/T advance, 70% against scanned B/L. Never 100% advance to a new supplier
- [ ] Third-party inspection agreed in writing before balance payment
