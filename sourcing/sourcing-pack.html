Five things that decide everything else

Read these before the detail. Four of the five cost nothing but paperwork and attention, and they matter more than any price you will negotiate.

01

Get GSTIN and IEC now

Your website still shows GSTIN: __. Without it the 18% IGST you pay at customs is a dead cost instead of input credit. IEC is ₹500 and takes two days. Nothing else in this pack works until both exist.

02

Diwali 2026 cannot come from China

Dhanteras is 6 November. A China order placed today lands after it. Buy this season's jhalar and ladi in India, record what sells, and use that record to buy from China in April 2027.

03

BIS belongs to the factory, not to you

LED bulbs, luminaires and lighting chains clear Indian customs only if that Chinese factory holds a BIS R-number for that exact model. Ask it in your first message, every time.

04

Multiply the FOB price by 1.4

That is roughly what a piece costs standing in your shop after duty, freight and clearing. Import only where the Delhi market rate is at least 1.6× that number.

30% advance, 70% against the B/L

The plan

Three ways to buy cheaper, in the order you should do them

Direct import gives the biggest margin and the worst cash flow. A new shop dies from blocked cash, not from thin margin — so tier one comes first, and tier three waits until you know exactly which ten items are worth it.

TierWhere you buyCash tied upMargin gainWhen

Tier one, in practice

Your own market is full of importers — Bhagirath Palace holds some of the largest lighting importers in North India, and a 200-piece lot out of a neighbour's container at importer+8% beats a bad direct import, paid in 30 days instead of 90. Beyond that: the assembly cluster in Noida, Greater Noida, Bahadurgarh and Sonipat for panels, COB, battens and drivers; Ahmedabad, Rajkot and Morbi for decorative, fancy and festive; Aligarh for fittings and hardware. Walk in with a printed list of your top twenty SKUs and monthly quantities — that list is what gets you factory rates instead of visitor rates.

Take one or two brand distributorships as well — Surya Roshni, Halonix, Syska, Wipro, Polycab, Havells. Margin is thinner at 12–20%, but branded stock brings contractors and RWA tenders through the door, and those buyers then take your unbranded high-margin lines too.

Before you import anything

The five registrations, and the one trap

GSTIN

Without it, 18% IGST at customs is a cost, not a credit. This is the single most valuable line in the pack.

IEC

Import Export Code from DGFT. Online, PAN-based, about ₹500, issued in one to two days, permanent.

AD Code

Your bank's Authorised Dealer letter, registered by your CHA at the port or ICD you clear through. No AD code, no bill of entry.

A CHA you trust

Ask three shops in the market who they use. ₹8,000–₹15,000 per consignment plus port charges.

Current account with A1 remittance

A branch that actually handles outward remittance to China without a two-week argument.

The trap: BIS registration sits with the Chinese factory

Under the Compulsory Registration Scheme, LED bulbs (IS 16102), LED luminaires (IS 10322:2026 series) and decorative lighting chains, fairy lights and rope lights (IS 10322 Part 5 Sec 7) can only be imported if that factory, for that exact model, holds a valid BIS R-number — obtained through an Authorised Indian Representative and tested in a BIS-recognised Indian lab. The number is issued to the manufacturing unit, never to the importer.

The whole LED luminaire family was revised to the IS 10322:2026 series and the concurrent-running transition period ended on 2 August 2026. Registration against only the old standard is now a problem at the port.

If the supplier has no R-number, your goods can be held, rejected or destroyed at your cost. So: ask for the number and the certificate copy in the first message, verify it yourself on the BIS site, and put the liability in writing in your purchase terms.

Also applies: BEE star labelling on lamps, and Legal Metrology labelling — importer name and address, MRP, month and year of import, country of origin — printed on the retail carton, not stuck on afterwards at the counter.

The rule of thumb: FOB rupee price × 1.32 to 1.45 on a small LCL shipment, dropping to about 1.25 on a full container. Import only where the Delhi wholesale rate is at least 1.6× your landed cost — below that, ninety days of blocked cash is not worth the difference. Check it per SKU, never per category.

Duty rates here are for planning. Confirm the exact BCD and IGST against the 8-digit HS code with your CHA on the day you file, and settle the 9405-versus-8539 classification for LED modules and lamps before the goods sail — that one has been litigated both ways.

The opening move

The enquiry email

Send it individually to each supplier, with their company name in the greeting — a mass email with twelve visible addresses gets deleted. Replies take three to seven working days. Follow up once after five days, then move on.

Main enquiry — send to factories

We are Goyal Lights, a lighting wholesaler and retailer at Bhagirath Palace, Chandni Chowk, New Delhi — the largest wholesale electrical market in India.
