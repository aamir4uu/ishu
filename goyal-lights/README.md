## Before deploying the landing page

`site/index.html` opens with an `EDIT ME` block. Replace the WhatsApp number,
phone number, shop number, GSTIN, Maps link and warranty window, then host it
free on Netlify, Cloudflare Pages or GitHub Pages and point your domain at it.

## The three things that matter most

1. **Google Business Profile, this week.** Free, and the highest-return asset a physical shop in Delhi has.
2. **WhatsApp Business with a real catalog and a 10-minute reply rule.** It will carry more revenue than every other channel combined.
3. **Be findable before the buyer reaches the lane.** In a market of 200 shops selling the same stock at the same post-bargain price, discoverability and reply speed are the only levers a new shop can actually pull.
