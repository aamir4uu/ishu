# Marketing & Sales Plan — Goyal Glow Lights

**Budget:** under ₹10,000/month · **Audience:** wholesale + retail, balanced
**Written:** 21 August 2026 · **Diwali:** Sunday 8 November 2026 (79 days out)

> Rename freely. Every mention of "Goyal Glow Lights" in this pack is a
> find-and-replace away from whichever name you pick in `02-naming-seo.md`.

---

## The strategy in four lines

1. Under ₹10,000/month you cannot outspend anyone. **You can out-appear them.**
2. So the plan is **free channels executed daily**, with cash reserved for one seasonal ad burst in Sept–Oct only.
3. Your differentiators are **reply speed, transport, GST billing, and replacement policy** — not price, not range.
4. You have **six weeks** before the Diwali wholesale money in this market has already been spent. Foundation work happens this week, not next month.

## 2. Google Business Profile — do this first, today

This is the highest-return free asset a physical shop in India has. Someone
standing in Chandni Chowk typing "light shop near me" is 30 seconds from your
counter with cash in hand.

**Setup**

- Create at [business.google.com](https://business.google.com), verify by postcard or video.
- **Name:** exactly your registered trade name — `Goyal Glow Lights`. Nothing more. Adding "Best LED Wholesale Delhi" gets profiles suspended.
- **Primary category:** `Lighting store`
- **Secondary categories:** `LED lighting supplier`, `Electrical supply store`, `Lighting contractor`, `Light bulb supplier`
- **Address:** full shop number, building name, lane, Bhagirath Palace, Chandni Chowk, Delhi 110006. Drag the map pin to the exact door — Old Delhi geocoding is bad and a pin 80 m away sends buyers to a competitor.
- **Hours:** Mon–Sat 11:00–19:00, **Sunday closed**. Add special hours for the Diwali week (see Section 6).
- **Attributes:** "In-store shopping", "Wheelchair accessible entrance" only if true, "Onsite services".
- **Buttons:** phone, WhatsApp, and your website URL.

**Photos — the part everyone skips and shouldn't**

Upload **30+ photos in week one**, then 4–5 every week forever. Profiles with
more photos get materially more direction requests. Shoot: shopfront with the
board readable, the lane approach from two directions so buyers can navigate,
interior wide shots, each product category, a lit-up night shot, your team, your
packing and bilty process, a GST bill with the numbers blurred out.

You sell lights. Your photos should be the best-lit photos in the entire market.
That is a free competitive advantage nobody in the lane is using.

**Weekly, 20 minutes**

- One **Post** per week: new stock arrival, a festive offer, a category spotlight.
- Answer **Q&A** — and seed 8–10 yourself from a second phone: *Do you sell wholesale? · Minimum order quantity? · Do you deliver outside Delhi? · Do you give GST bill? · Which day are you closed? · Do you have COB lights? · What is the warranty on LED?*
- Reply to **every review within 24 hours**, good or bad.

**Reviews — the actual ranking lever**

Target **30 reviews in 90 days**. Put a QR standee on the counter linking to
your review form. Ask every satisfied buyer, in person, at the moment they're
happy: *"Sir, agar theek laga ho to Google par ek review kar dijiye — bas 30
second lagenge."* Never buy reviews; Google strips them and can suspend you.

## 3. WhatsApp Business — this is your CRM, not just a chat app

Free app, and it will carry more of your revenue than every other channel here
combined.

**Setup (one evening)**

- Business profile: name, category, address, hours, website, email, and a description with your five differentiators.
- **Catalog:** load 100+ SKUs with clear photo, name, and *"Rate on enquiry"*. Group by collection: COB & Spotlights, Panel & Ceiling, Bulbs & Battens, Diwali & Decorative, Outdoor & Flood, Fancy & Chandeliers, Strips & Profiles, Accessories.
- **Labels:** `Retailer`, `Electrician`, `Decorator`, `Contractor`, `Retail`, `Quote sent`, `Order placed`, `Payment pending`, `Repeat`. This turns your inbox into a pipeline you can actually work.
- **Quick replies:** rate list, catalog link, shop location, transport info, GST/billing terms, warranty policy.
- **Greeting message** and **away message** (outside 11:00–19:00 and all Sunday).

**Broadcast lists** — up to 256 contacts each. Build `Retailers-UP`,
`Retailers-Haryana`, `Electricians-Delhi`, `Decorators`, `Retail-Customers`.
Send **one useful broadcast a week**, never more. New stock, a rate revision, a
festive combo. The moment you broadcast twice a week you get blocked and your
number's delivery quality drops.

**Status** — post 2–3 times daily. This is free reach to every contact you have
ever saved, and traders check status constantly. New arrivals, a carton being
packed, an order dispatched to Kanpur, a lit-up display.

**The rule that makes the money:** every WhatsApp enquiry gets a reply
**inside 10 minutes** during shop hours. In a market of 200 shops, being the
one that replies first is worth more than being the one that's ₹2 cheaper.

most common reason a local business fails to rank.

## 5. Content — ride the market-tour genre

"Bhagirath Palace light market" walkthrough videos with prices in the title are
already a large, active genre on YouTube pulling in buyers from across North
India. Those viewers are *planning a trip to your lane*. You do not need to
build an audience — you need to appear inside a search people are already doing.

**Format, 3–4 per week, 20–45 seconds, vertical, Hindi:**

1. **Price reveal** — hold the product, name it, name the wholesale rate, cut. Highest performing format in this genre, by a distance.
2. **New stock unboxing** — carton opens, lights go on.
3. **Lit vs unlit** — the same corner of the shop, switch flicked. This is what a lights shop should be posting and almost none do.
4. **How to choose** — "3000K vs 6500K — kaunsa kahan lagayein", "COB kitne watt ka chahiye", "jhalar ki wiring kaise check karein". These build trust with electricians.
5. **Order-to-dispatch** — packing, taping, bilty, truck. Sells your transport reliability better than any claim.

**Titles that carry the search:** `Bhagirath Palace Light Market 2026 | Diwali
Lights Wholesale Rate | COB LED Price List`. Put "Bhagirath Palace" in every
single title, description and caption — that is the query with real volume, and
you are physically inside the answer.

Post the same clip to Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and Facebook Reels. Pin
your shop location on every post. Add your WhatsApp link in the bio.

**Vlogger collaboration:** market-tour creators are always looking for shops to
feature. Offer access, a lit display, and honest on-camera rates. Costs you a
Sunday and some stock, not cash. One good feature can outrun three months of
your own posting.

### Weeks 3–4 · 4 – 17 Sep · **Wholesale push — the money window opens**

- [ ] **Rate list PDF v1** out on WhatsApp — festive range first, this is what retailers are shopping for right now
- [ ] Build the first 200 trade contacts into broadcast lists; send broadcast #1

- [ ] Walk the lane — introduce yourself to 20 neighbouring shops. In this market, shops refer overflow orders to each other. Being the new shop everyone knows is a distribution channel.
- [ ] First 8 reels live
- [ ] GBP Post twice a week from here on
- [ ] Ask every buyer for a Google review, every day, without exception

### Weeks 5–7 · 18 Sep – 8 Oct · **Peak stocking — maximum effort**
Out-of-Delhi retailers place their Diwali orders in this window. This is the
highest-leverage three weeks of your year.

- [ ] Daily WhatsApp Status — 3 posts a day, every day
- [ ] Weekly broadcast: "Diwali stock ready — jhalar, ladi, LED diya, motif. Rate list attached."
- [ ] Vlogger collaboration shoot
- [ ] **Meta ads ON** (see Section 7) — ₹200/day
- [ ] Combo/carton offers for retailers: mixed festive cartons at a set price simplify the buying decision and raise your average ticket
- [ ] Reels to 4/week

### Weeks 8–10 · 9 – 29 Oct · **Retail wave**
Navratri and Dussehra fall in this window — confirm the exact 2026 dates and
plan stock and staffing around them.

- [ ] Shift content from wholesale rates to home-decoration ideas
- [ ] GBP Posts: "Diwali lights in stock — Bhagirath Palace"
- [ ] Extended hours if the lane's footfall justifies it; update GBP special hours the same day
- [ ] Second staff member on WhatsApp so replies stay under 10 minutes
- [ ] Restock fast movers — track what sells out and reorder inside 48 hours

### Weeks 11–12 · 30 Oct – 12 Nov · **Diwali**
**Dhanteras Friday 6 Nov · Chhoti Diwali Saturday 7 Nov · Diwali Sunday 8 Nov.**

Note the calendar quirk: Diwali 2026 falls on a **Sunday**, your normal closed
day. **Friday 6th and Saturday 7th are therefore your two biggest retail days of
the year.** Staff, stock and cash-handling should be planned around that pair,
not around Sunday.

- [ ] Stop launching anything new. Pure fulfilment.
- [ ] Maximum staffing, extended hours, extra cash float and UPI capacity
- [ ] Special hours pushed to Google **a week in advance**
- [ ] Every single customer asked for a review — this fortnight can produce half your annual review count
- [ ] Photograph everything. This is next year's content library.

### Week 13+ · 13 – 19 Nov and beyond · **Convert the season into a business**

- [ ] Clearance on leftover festive stock — do not carry it 11 months
- [ ] Pivot content and stock to **wedding season** (Nov–Feb) and **Christmas/café lighting** (Nov–Dec)
- [ ] Broadcast to every buyer from the season with a thank-you and a wedding-season rate list
- [ ] Review the KPI sheet (Section 9). Whatever produced enquiries gets your Jan budget.

## 7. The one paid campaign — Meta ads, Sept–Oct only

Roughly **₹6,000 total across 30 days at ₹200/day**, running only in the peak
window. Off-season this is ₹0.

**Campaign A — Wholesale (₹120/day)**
Objective: **Messages → WhatsApp**. Location: Delhi NCR + Meerut, Ghaziabad,
Sonipat, Rohtak, Panipat, Karnal, Agra, Aligarh, Jaipur. Interests: retail
business owners, electrical contractors, event decorators. Creative: a carton
being packed, a bilty, a rate-list screenshot. Copy in Hinglish. CTA: "Rate list
WhatsApp par lijiye."

**Campaign B — Retail (₹80/day)**
Objective: **Messages** or **Traffic**. Location: 5 km radius around Chandni
Chowk. Age 25–55. Creative: the lit-vs-unlit clip. CTA: "Diwali lights —
Bhagirath Palace, direct wholesale rate."

Kill any ad set that hasn't produced an enquiry in ₹1,000 of spend. Put the
money into whichever one is working.

## 8. What to deliberately skip right now, and when to revisit

| Skip | Why | Revisit when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **IndiaMART paid** | ₹30k–1L+/year. It works for established wholesalers, but it will swallow your entire annual budget in one payment. | Free listing has produced 20+ genuine leads and you're converting them |
| **JustDial paid** | Same logic, plus heavy sales pressure at signup | Free listing is visibly producing calls |
| **Google Search ads** | "LED lights wholesale Delhi" is a competitive, expensive query. Your GBP covers the same intent for free. | Monthly ad budget crosses ₹25,000 |
| **Amazon / Flipkart / Meesho** | Adds packing, returns, ratings management and e-commerce GST compliance. It is a second business, not a channel. | After Diwali, if you have spare hands in Dec–Feb |
| **Website with cart** | Wholesale buyers negotiate; they will not check out online. A landing page plus WhatsApp converts better. | If retail D2C ever exceeds 30% of turnover |
| **Paid influencers** | Barter works fine in this niche | Never, at this budget |
| **SEO agency** | At ₹10k/month you'd be paying for reports, not rankings. Your GBP and content do the work. | Above ₹50k/month |
