## How these are scored

| Column | What it measures | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **KW** | Does the name itself contain a search term (`lights`, `LED`, `lighting`)? | Your Google Business Profile name is a ranking signal for local pack results. A name that legitimately contains "Lights" helps. Keyword-stuffing a GBP name you don't actually trade under is a policy violation and gets profiles suspended — so the keyword has to be in your **real** registered trade name. |
| **DIS** | Distinctiveness — can you own page 1 for your own brand name? | If a customer Googles your shop and finds ten other shops, you've lost the enquiry. |
| **DOM** | Likelihood of a short, clean `.com` / `.in` being free | Short domains get typed correctly, print smaller on a card, and read on a signboard. |
| **SAY** | Can a buyer say it on a phone call, spell it into a search bar, and read it in Devanagari on your board? | Half your buyers will voice-search or type in Hinglish. |
| **TM** | Trademark strength in Class 11 (lighting apparatus) and Class 35 (retail/wholesale) | Descriptive words are weak marks. The surname is what makes a composite mark registrable. |

Scores are out of 5. **Total is out of 25.**

## Names to avoid outright

| Avoid | Reason |
| --- | --- |
| **Goyal Electricals** / **Goyal Electricals & Electronics** | Already trading in Bhagirath Palace since 2007 with its own site, `goyalshop.com`. You would fund their brand searches. |
| **Goyal Electric Works** | Already exists in Chandni Chowk with its own site. |
| Anything with **Surya** | Surya Roshni Ltd is a large listed Indian lighting company. Direct trademark exposure. |
| Anything with **Ujala** | Existing FMCG brand — confusing recall. |
| **Goyal Havells / Philips / Syska** etc. | You are a dealer, not the brand. Using a supplier's name in your trade name is a trademark problem and most distributor agreements forbid it. |

### ⚠️ The "Roshni" caution

`Surya Roshni Ltd` is a major listed lighting manufacturer. "Roshni" on its own
is a generic Hindi word for light — generic terms are weak marks and hard for
anyone to monopolise — so **Goyal Roshni Lights** is very likely fine as a shop
name. But if you intend to register a trademark, run a TM search on
[ipindiaonline.gov.in](https://ipindiaonline.gov.in/tmrpublicsearch/) in Class 11
and Class 35 before you file, and expect a possible objection. Every other
name in this list is cleaner on that front.

## Recommendation

### 1st choice — **Goyal Glow Lights** (score 24/25)

- **Brand short form:** Goyal Glow · **Devanagari board:** गोयल ग्लो लाइट्स
- **Domain:** `goyalglow.com` (10 characters — check availability at your registrar; also grab `goyalglow.in`)
- **Handles:** `@goyalglow` on Instagram, YouTube, Facebook
- **GBP name:** Goyal Glow Lights
- **Tagline:** *COB, LED & Decorative Lights — Bhagirath Palace, Chandni Chowk*

**Why it wins on SEO specifically:**

1. **"Glow" is a rare brand token next to "Goyal"** — you will own page 1 for "Goyal Glow" within weeks of publishing, because nobody is competing for it. Compare with "Goyal Lights", where you fight an incumbent from day one.
2. **The keyword "Lights" sits in your genuine registered name**, so it appears legitimately in your Google Business Profile — no policy risk.
3. **Shortest clean domain in the list.** Fewer characters = fewer typos = fewer lost visits from a printed card or a signboard.
4. **Zero spelling ambiguity** in English or Hinglish. Voice search handles it. "Ujjwal" and "Illumination" do not.
5. **It spans both your audiences.** Festive buyers read it as warm; contractors read it as neutral. "Jagmag" leans festive, "Lumens" leans technical — you told me you want both.
6. **No collision, no trademark flag found.**

### 2nd choice — **Goyal Jagmag Lights** (23/25)
Pick this if you decide festive/decorative is going to be 70%+ of your business.
It is the most emotionally right name on the list for Diwali stock, and it is
just as clean online.

### 3rd choice — **Goyal Lightwala** (24/25 on score, 2nd on judgement)
Scores identically to Glow and is the most memorable name here. Ranked third
only because "wala" reads informal to project and contractor buyers, and you
asked for a balanced shop.

### If you want the traditional route — **Goyal Light House**
Understand the trade-off: strongest instant credibility inside the lane,
weakest position online. If your plan is walk-in and word-of-mouth only, it is a
defensible choice. Given you asked for SEO, it isn't the one I'd pick.

## Do these seven things before you print anything

1. **Domain check.** Search `goyalglow.com` and `goyalglow.in` at any registrar. Buy both plus `.co.in` if free. Budget ₹700–1,500/year total.
2. **Handle check.** Reserve `@goyalglow` on Instagram, YouTube, Facebook, and the WhatsApp Business display name — same day, even if you won't post for a month.
3. **Trademark search.** [IP India public search](https://ipindiaonline.gov.in/tmrpublicsearch/), Class 11 (lighting apparatus) and Class 35 (retail/wholesale services). Free to search. A TM application through an agent runs roughly ₹4,500–9,000 per class for an MSME.
4. **GST trade name.** Register the trade name on your GST certificate exactly as it will appear on your board and on Google. Mismatches cause Google Business Profile verification failures.
5. **Udyam / MSME registration.** Free, online, ~20 minutes. It reduces trademark filing fees and helps with bank credit later.
6. **Signboard in both scripts.** English on top, Devanagari below. The Devanagari line is what an out-of-state retailer will remember and repeat.
7. **One name everywhere.** Board, GST certificate, bill book, Google, WhatsApp, Instagram, IndiaMART, visiting card, transport bilty. Every mismatch splits your search authority.
