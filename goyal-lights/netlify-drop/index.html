Questions

Questions we get at the counter

Do you sell wholesale and retail both?

Yes, both from the same counter. Wholesale buyers get carton rates with minimum order quantities; retail buyers can buy single pieces at market rates.

What is the minimum order quantity?

Wholesale MOQs vary by item — send your list on WhatsApp and the rate reply includes the MOQ for each item. Retail has no minimum.

Do you deliver outside Delhi?

Yes. Orders are packed, labelled and booked with bilty to your city's transport office, with tracking details shared on WhatsApp.

Do you give a GST bill?

Yes, every order gets a full GST invoice with HSN codes, so your input credit stays clean.

Which day is the shop closed?

Sunday. The whole Bhagirath Palace market shuts on Sunday. We are open Monday to Saturday, 11:00 AM to 7:00 PM.

Can you see photos of stock before ordering?

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp and we send photos or a short video of the exact lot before you confirm the order.

Do you have COB lights for false ceiling?

Yes — recessed and surface COB spotlights from 7W to 30W, gimbal and anti-glare options, with drivers, in warm, natural and cool white.

How do I reach Bhagirath Palace from the metro?

Chandni Chowk metro (Yellow Line) or Lal Qila (Violet Line), then walk along Chandni Chowk road towards the Red Fort. The lane entrance is just before the cinema hall, part-hidden behind a tree and a temple.
