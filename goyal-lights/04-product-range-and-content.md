## 1. The nine categories

Every counter in the lane sells some mix of these. Categories 1–4 are your
volume, 8 is your margin, 9 is your hedge.

### 1. COB & Spot Lighting *(your headline category)*
COB spotlights 7W / 12W / 15W / 20W / 30W · surface-mounted COB · recessed COB ·
gimbal / tiltable spots · anti-glare deep-recessed · track lights · magnetic
track systems and rails · focus lights · drivers and SMPS units

**Buyers:** contractors, interior fitters, electricians, shop-fitters
**Season:** year-round, peaks Mar–Jun with renovation work

### 2. Panel, Ceiling & Cove
Round and square LED panels 3W–24W · backlit panels · edge-lit slim panels ·
surface panels · false-ceiling cove and profile lights · aluminium LED profiles
with diffusers · linear/architectural profiles

**Buyers:** contractors, electricians, office and retail fit-outs
**Season:** year-round

### 3. Bulbs, Battens & Tubes
LED bulbs 9W / 12W / 15W / 18W in B22 and E27 · 3-in-1 colour-changing bulbs ·
inverter and rechargeable bulbs · T5 and T8 tubes · battens 20W / 40W ·
high-bay and low-bay for godowns · filament and vintage Edison bulbs

**Buyers:** everyone. This is the repeat-purchase engine that brings buyers back.
**Season:** year-round, flat

### 4. Diwali & Decorative *(your peak-season revenue)*
Rice lights and fairy strings · LED jhalar · curtain lights · net lights ·
icicle lights · pixel strings with controller · ladi · rope light and neon flex ·
star lights and akash kandil · LED diyas and tea lights · LED toran · motif
lights and gate motifs · blinking controllers · RGB controllers and remotes

**Buyers:** retailers stocking up (Aug–Oct), households (Oct–Nov), decorators
**Season:** Aug–Nov is everything. Christmas/New Year gives a second, smaller run.

### 5. Outdoor & Architectural
Flood lights 10W–200W · street lights · gate and pillar lights · bollards ·
garden and landscape spikes · wall washers · underwater and pool lights ·
facade lighting

**Buyers:** contractors, RWAs, temple and gurudwara committees, farmhouse owners
**Season:** year-round, peaks with construction

### 6. Fancy & Interior Decorative *(highest margin per piece)*
Chandeliers and jhoomar · pendants and hanging lights · wall brackets and
sconces · mirror and vanity lights · picture lights · table and floor lamps ·
café and vintage cage lights · Turkish and Moroccan lamps

**Buyers:** households, cafés, restaurants, salons, interior designers
**Season:** year-round, wedding and housewarming seasons lift it

### 7. Solar
Solar street lights · solar garden and spike lights · solar decorative strings ·
solar gate lights

**Buyers:** RWAs, farmhouses, small municipalities, rural retailers
**Note:** the fastest-growing sub-category in the reports, and thinly stocked in
most lane shops. A genuine gap worth taking.

### 8. Accessories & Consumables *(your attach-sale margin)*
Drivers and SMPS · holders and adaptors · strip connectors · dimmers · RGB
controllers and remotes · PVC pipe and channel · switches, sockets, plug-tops ·
MCBs · wire and tape

Nobody comes to buy these, and almost everybody leaves with them. Keep them at
the counter, not at the back. This category quietly carries your daily margin.

### 9. Event & Party Lighting *(the segment most shops ignore)*
DJ and party lights · LED PAR bars · disco balls · strobes · laser projectors ·
fog machines · rental-grade pixel controller sets

**Buyers:** tent houses, event companies, DJs, banquet halls
**Why it matters:** it is counter-seasonal to Diwali and runs hard through the
wedding months. Few lane shops carry it properly. Stocking it evens out your
cash flow between festivals.

## 2. Stock strategy for a new shop

| Approach | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Go deep, not wide, in one category** | Be *the* COB shop or *the* jhalar shop. A new shop that stocks a little of everything is forgettable; a new shop that always has the size you need in one category gets referred. |
| **Carry both grades, and say which is which** | Budget import and branded Indian side by side, honestly labelled. The shop that tells a retailer "yeh sasta hai lekin 6 mahine chalega, yeh mehnga hai lekin warranty ke saath" gets the second order. |
| **Stock the brands buyers already search** | Philips, Havells, Syska, Wipro, Crompton, Bajaj, Orient, Polycab, Surya, Halonix. Buyers search brand names, then find your shop. |
| **Track sell-out speed, not stock value** | In an 11-week festive window, a fast mover that's out of stock costs more than slow stock costs to hold. Reorder inside 48 hours. |
| **Write a written replacement policy** | "LED fail ho jaye to __ months tak counter par replacement." Put it on your board, your bill, your website and your WhatsApp profile. It is the market's biggest unspoken complaint and almost nobody addresses it publicly. |

## 4. Twelve content pieces that will carry the first quarter

Shoot these in two Sunday sessions. Each one is a reel, a YouTube Short, a
Facebook Reel, a WhatsApp Status and a Google Business Post — five placements
from one clip.

1. **Diwali lights wholesale rate — 10 items, 10 prices.** The single highest-performing format in this niche.
2. **Lit vs unlit** — same shelf, switch flicked. Proves your stock, sells the feeling.
3. **How to reach us from Chandni Chowk metro** — walking POV. Solves a real problem; the lane entrance is genuinely hard to find.
4. **3000K vs 4000K vs 6500K** — same room, three temperatures. Educational, gets saved and shared, builds electrician trust.
5. **COB kitne watt ka chahiye** — room size to wattage, simple rule of thumb.
6. **Jhalar quality check** — how to spot bad wiring before you buy a carton. Honesty content; it converts wholesale buyers.
7. **Order packing to bilty** — carton, tape, label, transport. Sells reliability to out-of-Delhi buyers.
8. **New stock unboxing** — repeatable weekly, zero planning.
9. **₹500 mein poora ghar** — a full Diwali decoration budget breakdown. Retail gold.
10. **Chandelier walkthrough** — slow pan, lights on. High-margin category, and it looks expensive on camera.
11. **Ek din dukaan par** — behind the counter. Builds the personal trust this market runs on.
12. **Customer ka order ready hai** — with permission. Social proof beats every claim you make about yourself.

## 5. What to fill into the landing page before you deploy it

`site/index.html` has an `EDIT ME` block at the very top. Replace:

| Placeholder | Replace with |
| --- | --- |
| `91XXXXXXXXXX` | Your WhatsApp number with country code, no `+` or spaces — appears in three places |
| `+91 XXXXX XXXXX` | Display version of your phone number |
| `Shop No. __` | Your exact shop number and building name |
| `GSTIN: __` | Your GSTIN once registered |
| `MAPS_LINK` | Your Google Business Profile short link, once verified |
| `__ months` | Your actual replacement/warranty window |
| Category items | Trim to what you genuinely stock — an empty promise on the site becomes a refund at the counter |

Then deploy free on Netlify, Cloudflare Pages or GitHub Pages and point
`goyalglow.com` at it.
