## The five things that matter most

1. **GSTIN first.** Lights are a taxable category; Meesho's non-GST route does not cover them. Same GSTIN the import plan needs.
2. **List Diwali strings by 15 September.** QC takes up to 3 days and ranking takes two weeks. Dhanteras is 6 November.
3. **Choose "Only wrong/defective returns."** Decor lights under "All returns" come back at 15–20% after the function. The calculator prices both.
4. **Image 1 is the real product on pure white.** AI cleans the background; it never invents the product. Carton label with BIS R-number is image 5.
5. **Weigh the packed box and dispatch next day.** Weight mismatches and late dispatch (₹50/order) are the two silent leaks.

## What I could not do from here

- **Control your PC or the Supplier Panel.** This runs in a cloud sandbox. Registration, image upload, catalog submit and label printing are yours; `06-weekly-workflow.md` §F explains the local-agent setup if you want that automated.
- **Render images.** The connected image generator has 0 credits on a free plan. Prompts are ready for ChatGPT; send raw photos plus credits and I run them.
- **Download Meesho's actual bulk templates** (login-only). Send me the category templates and I fill them from the listings sheet.
