## 2. Registration steps (do this on your PC)

1. Go to `supplier.meesho.com` → **Start Selling**.
2. Enter mobile → OTP → email → password.
3. **GSTIN & PAN.** Enter GSTIN; Meesho auto-fills the legal name and principal address. If the shop is not the principal place of business, add it as an additional place in GST first, otherwise pickup verification fails.
4. **Pickup address.** Pincode 110006, shop number, floor, market name, landmark ("opposite Red Fort side entry").
5. **Bank details.** Account in the name that matches the GST certificate.
6. **Store name.** `Goyal Lights` (this is what buyers see on the product page).
7. Accept the supplier agreement. Approval is usually same day to 48 hours.
8. Install the **Meesho Supplier app** on the phone that will be at the shop; orders, dispatch labels and pickup alerts arrive there.

## 3. Settings to fix before the first listing

- **Dispatch SLA.** Default is next-day dispatch. Keep it. Late dispatch is ₹50/order and hurts ranking more than the fine.
- **Holiday mode.** Turn on for Diwali closing days; an order that can't be dispatched is a cancellation penalty (₹25) *and* a ranking hit.
- **Return policy per catalog.** For lights: *Only Wrong/Defective Returns*. Buyers of decor lights return at 15–20% under "All Returns" (change of mind after the function). The price you enter for this option is what the buyer sees.
- **Packaging.** Meesho does not supply boxes. Buy 5-ply corrugated boxes in three sizes (see `06-weekly-workflow.md`) and bubble wrap. Bulb and chandelier breakages are your loss without an unboxing video from the buyer.

## 4. Money — how Meesho pays

- **Commission: 0%.** Real deductions are shipping (weight-slab based, system-set, plus 18% GST on the fee), return/RTO reverse-shipping when a parcel comes back, and penalties. Expect 12–20% of the selling price to disappear on a ₹300–₹500 lighting order; the calculator in `03-pricing-calculator.xlsx` works it out per SKU.
- **Payout** lands in the bank ~7 days after delivery (weekly cycle). Returns are deducted from the next cycle.
- **TCS:** Meesho deducts 0.5% TCS under GST and 1% TDS under Income Tax on the gross; both show as credits in your returns. Your CA needs the monthly Meesho tax report.

## 5. Compliance on lighting specifically

- **BIS CRS.** LED bulbs (IS 16102), LED luminaires (IS 10322:2026 series) and lighting chains (IS 10322 Pt 5 Sec 7) must carry a BIS R-number on the product and carton. Meesho shows a "product may be regulated by BIS QCO" note on these pages, and QC can ask for the certificate. **List only items where the carton shows the R-number**, and photograph that marking as image 5. Unmarked imports risk delisting and a customer complaint you cannot defend.
- **Legal Metrology** on the retail carton: MRP, net quantity, importer/packer name and address, month/year, country of origin, customer care number. Your supplier's carton must carry this; if it does not, a printed sticker before dispatch is mandatory.
- **Voltage and warranty** claims in the listing must match the carton. Do not write "1 year warranty" unless you will honour it by replacement.
