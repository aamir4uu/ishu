## Note on the title capitalisation

Strict APA lowercases prepositions of three letters or fewer, which would make
it "Cuts 10 Hours off Every Project". Here "off" is the particle of the phrasal
verb "cuts off", and APA's own guidance treats particles as major words, so the
capital "Off" in the supplied title stands. Flagging it so nobody "corrects"
it either way.

## Brand guide compliance

- Written for Sitejet Studio only. Team sharing, roles, client portal,
  ticketing and white label appear only in the Sitejet Studio section.
- Leads with efficiency and scale, per the Studio key messages: "the more sites
  you take on, the more the same five templates pay back".

- No "drag and drop", "no coding required", or "stunning websites".

## Product claims to spot-check

`help.sitejet.io`, `www.sitejet.io` and `community.sitejet.io` were blocked from
the environment that produced this draft, so the product facts came from help
centre and community excerpts returned by search. Confirm the exact UI labels
against the live builder before publishing:

- "Actions" menu on a website, then "Create as template"; saved templates sit
  under Templates in the top menu bar, in "My templates".
- Templates are built from swappable section presets.
- Burger menu, "Copy & Share Websites", generates a "Perpetual" or "One-time"
  code that the recipient pastes into "Copy Website". This shipped in the
  CW 11.2024 release.

Two claims in the Sitejet Studio section lean on the brand guide's feature list
rather than on a help article: that templates in a Sitejet Studio account are
available to the whole team, and that roles can restrict who overwrites them.
Both follow from multi-user roles and permission controls, but confirm the
wording against how the product actually behaves.
