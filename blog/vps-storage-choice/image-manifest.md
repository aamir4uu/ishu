## Important: the files still need downloading and uploading

This draft was produced in a sandboxed environment where outbound access to
`pexels.com` and `images.pexels.com` was blocked by the network egress proxy.
The image **files** could not be downloaded or re-hosted. Both URLs below were
confirmed through search and point at a real Pexels photo page, but the
`![...]()` markdown in the draft currently points at the photo page rather than
at an uploaded asset.

Before publishing, for each image: open the source URL, download the file,
upload it to the SolusVM CMS media library, and replace the URL inside the
`![alt text](...)` markdown with the uploaded asset path. Leave the "Image
Source" link underneath pointing at the original Pexels page. That is what the
brief asks for and it is also what the Pexels licence expects.

Both are Pexels, free to use commercially with no attribution required. The
credit line is there because the client asked for it, not because the licence
demands it.
