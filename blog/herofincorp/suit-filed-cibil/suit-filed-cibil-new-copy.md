## How Does a CIBIL Suit Filed Impact Your Credit Score?

A CIBIL suit filed remark hurts in two ways, and the second is the one people miss.

- Months of missed payments sit behind it, and they've already dragged the score down, usually well under the 700 most lenders want.
- Because the flag sits in the account status, a lender's policy filter can decline you before anyone looks at the score.
- Any credit that does come is priced for the risk, so higher rates and lower limits.
- And it stays until the lender updates it, however long you wait.

## Types of Suit Filed Status in a CIBIL Report

Each account's 'Suit Filed / Wilful Default' field carries one of four values. Written off and settled sit in a separate field and can appear alongside, which is why suit filed, wilful default and written off turn up together on one report and mean three different things.

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| No Suit Filed | The default value. No legal action has been reported. |
| Suit Filed | The lender has filed a recovery case, in a civil court, a Lok Adalat, or a Debt Recovery Tribunal for dues of Rs 20 lakh and above. |
| Wilful Default | The lender holds that you could pay and didn't, or diverted the funds. Under RBI's 2024 directions the tag needs a committee process and a chance to respond, and applies to dues of Rs 25 lakh and above. |
| Suit Filed (Wilful Default) | Both at once: a case in court and a wilful default classification. The hardest to shift. |

## Difference Between Suit Filed, Written-off, and Settled Status

These three remarks describe different stages of the same bad debt, and lenders read them differently.

| Status | What happened | Is the debt still owed? | How lenders read it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Suit Filed | Legal recovery action has begun | Yes, plus legal costs | An active default. Usually an automatic decline. |
| Written-off | The lender has written the dues off in its books, typically after 180 days or more of non-payment | Yes. The lender can still recover it. | A serious default. Some lenders will consider you once it's paid. |
| Settled | You paid an agreed amount lower than the full dues and the account was closed | No | Negative, but the softest of the three. The score recovers as newer accounts stay clean. |

Order matters here. Suit filed accounts can later show written-off, and a written-off account can end as 'Post (WO) Settled' once you pay part of what was owed. Only full payment gets you to the clean end state, which is 'Closed' with a zero balance and no remarks. Settled clears the debt but leaves the record.

## Conclusion

A CIBIL suit filed remark isn't a verdict, but it won't leave on its own. Pull the report, find the account, clear or settle it, collect the closure letter and raise the dispute, then check the report again in 30 days. That sequence takes a month or two if the lender cooperates, and it is the only thing that gets the flag off; every loan you apply for in the meantime is priced against it.

### What Is a Suit Filed in CIBIL?

It's an account status showing your lender has started legal proceedings to recover overdue dues, reported to TransUnion CIBIL along with the date and the amount. There may be no ruling yet; the entry only says a case exists.

### Does a Suit Filed Status Reduce CIBIL Score?

Yes, though indirectly. CIBIL doesn't deduct a fixed number of points for the remark itself; the score falls because of the months of non-payment that led to it, and it stays low while the balance sits unpaid. Then the flag does separate damage. Many lenders decline any report carrying it, whatever the score says. So the account has to be fixed before the score can be.

### What's the Difference Between Suit Filed and Written-off?

Suit filed means the lender is still chasing the money through the courts. Written-off means it has stopped expecting to recover it in its accounts, usually after 180 days of non-payment. One account can carry both, and both stay until you clear the dues.

### Can I Get a Loan While a Suit Filed Entry Is Active?

It's rare, and the terms are poor when it happens. Most lenders' policies treat the remark as an automatic decline; the few that lend price for it, and price hard. Clear the dues and get the entry updated first; approvals and rates improve within one or two reporting cycles.

### What Documents Do I Need to Raise a Dispute for Removal?

Your CIBIL report with the entry marked, the No Dues Certificate or loan closure letter from the lender, proof of the final payment or the settlement letter, and your PAN. If the lender filed the suit in error, add the written communication that says so. CIBIL confirms with the lender before changing anything.

### How to Clear a Suit Filed in CIBIL?

Pay or settle the account with the lender, take a No Dues Certificate, then raise a dispute on cibil.com under Credit Report Help and attach the certificate. Your lender has 21 days to confirm and CIBIL nine more to update. Past 30 days, RBI's compensation rule pays you Rs 100 a day for the delay.
