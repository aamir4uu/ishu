## Corrections to the live copy

- **"The defaulted loan will remain on your credit report for up to seven years."** No Indian rule provides that; the entry stays until the lender updates it. Dropped.
- **"Insolvency proceedings under the IBC for defaults of Rs 1 crore and above."** The Rs 1 crore threshold is the corporate insolvency threshold; IBC individual insolvency is notified only for personal guarantors to corporate debtors. Dropped.
- **"Section 420 of the Indian Penal Code."** The IPC was replaced on 1 July 2024; cheating is now Section 318 of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita. Updated.
- The em dash and curly quotes are gone.
