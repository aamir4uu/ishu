## What Is e-KYC?

The e-KYC full form is electronic Know Your Customer. e-KYC is a remote, paperless process used by lending institutions and [NBFCs](https://www.herofincorp.com/blog/what-is-nbfc) to verify the identity and address of an applicant digitally.

Unlike traditional methods that rely on physical document submission, e-KYC uses the Aadhaar system to pull authenticated data directly from the Unique Identification Authority of India ([UIDAI](https://uidai.gov.in/)). The data comes straight from UIDAI, so it's current and can't be edited on the way.

### The Core Pillars of e-KYC

- Digital Identity: Verifying your credentials through the Aadhaar database.
- Paperless Verification: Eliminating the need for physical photocopies or manual form filling.
- Real-Time Authentication: Providing instant confirmation of identity to speed up loan approvals.

## Types of e-KYC Methods in India

To cater to different levels of digital access and security requirements, there are four ways to complete your e-KYC:

<!-- EDIT: "two primary ways" changed to "four ways" now that offline e-KYC and Video KYC are listed below. -->

### 1. OTP-Based e-KYC

This is the most common method for an instant Personal Loan.

- How it works: A One-Time Password (OTP) is sent to your Aadhaar-registered mobile number.
- Benefit: It is entirely remote and requires no specialised hardware beyond your phone.

### 2. Biometric-Based e-KYC

Often used for high-value transactions or sensitive financial services.

- How it works: Verification is completed using your fingerprints or iris scans.
- Benefit: It provides a higher level of security because the person has to be physically present at a designated point of service.

<!-- NEW SECTION START: Offline e-KYC. Brief: H3, informational, about 50 words, keyword "ekyc meaning", reference Tata Capital. -->
### 3. Offline e-KYC

Offline e-KYC keeps your Aadhaar number out of the lender's database. Download the digitally signed XML file from myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in, lock it with a four-digit share code and hand the file to the lender, which verifies UIDAI's digital signature on it offline. It carries your name, address, photo and a reference ID, and no call to UIDAI's servers happens at the lender's end.
<!-- NEW SECTION END -->

<!-- NEW SECTION START: Video KYC. Brief: H3, informational, about 50 words, keyword "ekyc meaning". -->
### 4. Video KYC

Video KYC is a live call with a person on the other end. An officer of the lender records the session, checks your PAN on camera, matches your face to your Aadhaar photo and geotags your location, all under RBI's V-CIP rules. It's the route to full KYC without a branch visit, and it lifts the Rs 60,000-a-year loan cap that OTP-only KYC carries.

## Eligibility and Requirements for e-KYC

To ensure a smooth digital journey, applicants must meet specific criteria before starting the process, and while e-KYC is widely accessible, having your digital "house in order" is essential.

## e-KYC Process Explained Step by Step

Whichever app you're in, the e-KYC process runs the same way, because the data comes from one place.

1. You give consent, which the lender has to record before it touches your Aadhaar details.
2. You enter your Aadhaar number or a Virtual ID.
3. UIDAI challenges you: an OTP on the registered mobile, or a fingerprint or iris scan on a registered device.
4. On a match, UIDAI returns your name, date of birth, gender, address and photo to the lender. That is all it sends; your biometrics never leave UIDAI.
5. Next, the lender matches the name to your PAN, screens the record and stores it.
6. A CKYC identifier is created or updated, so the next institution can reuse the record instead of repeating it.

## How to Apply for e-KYC Online

How to apply for e-KYC online depends on what you're opening. For a loan or a bank account there's no separate application; e-KYC is a step inside the form. Investments route through a KYC Registration Agency instead.

Loan or bank account:

1. Start the application in the lender's app or website.
2. Keep the Aadhaar-linked mobile with you. That's where the OTP goes, whatever number you typed on the form.
3. Enter your Aadhaar, approve the OTP and tick the consent screen.
4. Confirm PAN if asked, then e-sign the agreement.

Mutual funds or a demat account:

1. Go to a SEBI-registered KRA such as CVL KRA or CAMS KRA, or through your broker or fund house.
2. Complete Aadhaar OTP or Video KYC and upload a PAN copy.
3. Track the status on the KRA site with your PAN. Validation usually takes a few working days.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Is There Any Fee for Completing e-KYC?

No, the e-KYC process is a free service provided by authorised financial institutions and platforms.

### Can I Do e-KYC Without an Aadhaar Card?

No. An Aadhaar number is the mandatory foundation for the e-KYC process in India to verify identity and link your mobile number.

### What if I Don't Receive the OTP?

Make sure your mobile number is correctly linked to your Aadhaar and that you have a stable network. If the issue persists, you may need to update your details at an Aadhaar Enrolment Centre or contact your loan provider's support.

### Is e-KYC Only for Personal Loans?

While it is essential for loans, it is also used for opening bank accounts, buying insurance, investing in mutual funds, and other financial services.

### What Is the Full Form of e-KYC?

Electronic Know Your Customer. KYC is the identity and address check RBI requires every regulated lender to run before it opens an account or sanctions a loan. Adding the "e" means it's done digitally, against UIDAI's Aadhaar records or over a live video, so nobody photocopies anything.

### What Is the Difference Between Aadhaar e-KYC and Video KYC?

Aadhaar e-KYC is machine to machine. You approve an OTP or press a fingerprint, and UIDAI returns your details in seconds. Video KYC puts a person in the loop: a lender's officer sees you live, checks your PAN on camera and records the call. Aadhaar e-KYC is faster; Video KYC is what lenders use when the Aadhaar-linked mobile isn't with you, or when the loan is bigger than an OTP-only KYC allows.

### Why Does e-KYC Sometimes Fail?

Nine times out of ten it's the mobile number. If the one on your Aadhaar is old or inactive, the OTP never arrives. Other common causes: a name spelt differently on PAN and Aadhaar (Mohd against Mohammed), biometrics you locked on the mAadhaar app and forgot about, or three wrong OTP entries in a row. Update the Aadhaar record at an enrolment centre, then try again.

### Is e-KYC Mandatory for a Personal Loan?

KYC itself is mandatory; doing it electronically isn't. Under RBI's KYC Master Direction the lender must verify you, and it can do that through Aadhaar OTP, biometrics, Video KYC, a DigiLocker document or a physical form at a branch. For an instant personal loan, though, e-KYC is the only route that gets money to you the same day.
