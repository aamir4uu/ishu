## Note on the title capitalisation

APA title case lowercases minor conjunctions of three letters or fewer, and
"so" is on that list alongside and, as, but, for, if, nor, or and yet. The
headline is therefore set as "What to Send a Client on Launch Day **so** They
Never Email You Again". That is APA-correct, not a typo. If the client prefers
the visual look of "So", capitalise it and the piece departs from strict APA in
that one place. Flagging it because a lowercase "so" mid-headline reads as an
error to people who are not looking for the APA rule.
