## Findings

**Imperative step runs are confirmed.** Steps 2 and 3 of the same four-item
list were highlighted in both the 37.9% and the 22.9% reports, while the prose
around the list stayed clean each time. Two independent sightings, so the
pattern is promoted out of the candidates table and into the script as a
sixteenth gate. The threshold allows a run of two consecutive list items
opening on a bare command and fails on three.

**A bare definitional copula is a tell.** "The e-KYC full form is electronic
Know Your Customer." Six words, zero surprise, and the single most predictable
sentence on the page. Definitions invite this shape, which is why glossary
pages score badly. The fix is to drop the copula and name the thing.

**"The real reason" is the same trope as "the real question".** The
persuasive_authority family had the question form and not the reason form.
Added.

**Pronoun-subject parallels flag.** "It delivers X, and it lifts Y." Two verbs
hung off one repeated pronoun with a comma and an "and" between them. Logged
as a candidate pending a second sighting.

**Headings can be highlighted, and some of them cannot be changed.** The FAQ
heading "What Is the Difference Between Aadhaar e-KYC and Video KYC?" is
mandated verbatim by the client's content brief. It was left alone. Logged in
the known-conflicts table.
